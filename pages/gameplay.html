Gameplay

Explore different activities such as fishing, bug catching, and diving as you discover the beauty and calm of island life.

Start Your Adventure!

🐟Fishing

Take a moment to relax and enjoy the peaceful art of fishing. Whether you're casting your line at sunrise or waiting patiently by the shore, every catch brings a little joy and a new discovery.

🍎Catching

Wander through the island and keep a close eye on your surroundings—bugs can appear when you least expect it.

🫧Diving

Dive beneath the ocean's surface and explore a hidden world full of mystery and rare sea creatures. As you swim deeper, keep an eye out for bubbles—each dive could lead to an exciting discovery waiting just below the waves!

🍃Designing

Bring your dream island to life by shaping every detail to match your style. From placing furniture and decorating outdoor spaces to building bridges, paths, and custom layouts, designing your island lets you create a peaceful world that feels uniquely yours.

Welcome friends into your world, explore new islands, and create memorable moments together through a relaxing and social gameplay experience.

Invite villagers who match your style and create a community filled with different personalities, stories, and friendships.
