Frequently Asked Questions

What is Animal Crossing: New Horizons?

It's a life-simulation game where you move to a deserted island and build your own community. You can decorate, fish, catch bugs, and interact with villagers at your own pace.

Can you win the game?

No, the game has no ending. You can play freely and keep improving your island however you like.

Is Animal Crossing multiplayer?

Yes, it supports both local and online multiplayer, so you can play with friends

What platforms is the game available on?

It is available exclusively on the Nintendo Switch™ and Switch lite. However, if you want to play co-op, you will need a joy-con or a Nintendo Switch Pro Controller for each player.

How do you get more villagers?

You can invite villagers by visiting mystery islands, using Amiibo cards, or waiting for random villagers to move in.

How do I make money fast?

You can earn Bells by fishing, catching bugs, selling fruit, or trading items like turnips.

Can friends visit my island?

Yes, you can invite friends locally or online to explore your island together.

What are seasonal events?

The game features events based on real-world seasons and holidays, adding new items and activities throughout the year.

How many users can be registered on one island?

Animal Crossing: New Horizons supports the registering of up to 8 users on a single Nintendo Switch™ console with a single copy of the game, all of whom can share a single island.
